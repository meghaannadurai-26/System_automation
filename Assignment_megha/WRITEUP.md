# Arm health system - write-up

**Why these stop limits.** Torque limit is 120% of each joint's rated torque (so 180 / 120 / 54 Nm for shoulder / elbow / wrist), because a small joint like the wrist would never reach a single fixed number picked for the shoulder. Temperature is 100 C and vibration 12 mm/s for every joint. These sit clearly above what my healthy data produces (healthy torque is about 60% of rated), so they only fire for real danger. `safety.py` has no import from the model and does only comparisons. Invalid values (None, NaN, negative) also trigger a stop.

**How model and log combine.** A logistic regression (written by hand with gradient descent, no libraries) is trained on 150 joint readings (torque as a fraction of rated, temperature, vibration). The arm's wear probability is the worst of its three joints. The log scanner works clause by clause, so "no leak, jerky motion" only counts the second part. Words have three levels (critical 1.0, medium 0.5, low 0.2) and each note fades with a 7-day half-life, so an old note matters less. The log risk is `1 - exp(-sum)`. Combined risk = 0.6 * model + 0.4 * log. Under 0.25 is SAFE, 0.25-0.55 is MONITOR, above 0.55 is SCHEDULE_MAINTENANCE. A critical note within 14 days (burning smell, crack, smoke...) together with model probability >= 0.5 gives EMERGENCY_STOP. If the model fails to load the code falls back to the log only.

**Spoof defense.** This one looks at time, not at other joints. The system keeps the last 6 samples per joint and checks three things: (1) torque is frozen (range under 0.2 Nm) while temperature or vibration still move, which is what a replayed or stuck value looks like, (2) torque jumps by more than 30% of rated between two samples, and (3) torque falls by 20% of rated while temperature and vibration both rise, which is what hiding a real problem looks like. If anything fires, the arm is at least SCHEDULE_MAINTENANCE, and becomes EMERGENCY_STOP if the model or the log also looks bad. It would catch frozen/replayed values, crude jumps and "torque goes down while the joint heats up" masking. It would NOT catch a slow, noisy fake that moves smoothly and stays plausible, or an attacker who also fakes temperature and vibration.
